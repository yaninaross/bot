## Key Observations

1. **Clear step function at 09:30**: pre-market plateau (~6.8%, Sharpe 0.54) jumps to post-open plateau (~8.3%, Sharpe 0.66). RTH bars give much better ranking signal than PM bars.
2. **Sweet spot 09:31-09:37**: avg PnL 8.1-8.6%, Sharpe 0.66-0.70. Best risk-adjusted at 09:37 (Sharpe 0.696).
3. **No penalty for waiting past open**: scanning at 09:34-09:37 beats 09:30 despite shrinking entry window. Better ranking info outweighs missed early peaks.
4. **LH→FB transition visible**: at 09:37, 20 LH + 9 FB. By 09:42, only 8 LH + 21 FB. By 09:45, 100% fallback (0 LH). Later scan = more fallback entries.
5. **FB quality improves with T**: at 09:15, FB avg = +1.44%. At 09:37, FB avg = +2.66%. At 09:45 (all FB), avg = +7.77%. Later fallback entries catch the fade at a lower price.
6. **LH quality also improves with T**: LH avg rises from +9.50% (09:15) to +13.12% (09:41). Fewer but better peaks when scanning later.
7. **Trade count drops**: 32 trades at 09:30 → 28 at 09:45. Later scan = more no-pick days.
8. **Agreement drops**: 09:15-09:27 cluster agrees ~93-100%. 09:30+ agrees only ~69-83% with pre-market. Different ranking = different picks.

## Operational Implications

- **09:34 is the pragmatic optimum**: best raw avg (+8.65%), strong Sharpe (0.685), still 23 LH entries (vs 20 at baseline). 4 minutes after open gives time for grouped daily fetch + ranking.
- **09:37 is the Sharpe optimum**: 0.696 Sharpe, but only 20 LH entries and 5 no-pick days.
- **Budget warning**: current live scanner runs at 09:15 (6:15 PST). Moving to 09:34 requires the full pipeline (grouped daily, gap rank, CS lookup, float filter) to complete in ~4 minutes after open. CS lookups need batching.
- **09:45 pure-fallback is viable**: +7.77% avg, 0.646 Sharpe — all from bar-15 close entries. No local_high scanning needed. Simplest possible strategy.
